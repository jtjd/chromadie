# Droplets on Glass source

Source clip: [Rain Drops on Glass](https://www.pexels.com/video/rain-drops-on-glass-9967479/)
by Nishant Aneja, accessed 2026-08-04.

The 16:9 source was converted into a black-backed grayscale highlight plate:
the droplet highlights are retained while the cloudy pane is suppressed. A
24-second segment uses a 1.5-second crossfade from its end back into its start,
so the profile receives a quiet, continuous loop rather than a hard reset.
The video is screen-blended at low opacity and never darkens the user's
profile background. The poster is the reduced-motion fallback.
