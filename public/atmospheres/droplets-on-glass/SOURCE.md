# Droplets on Glass source

Source clip: [Rain Droplets on Windshield Close-Up](https://www.pexels.com/video/rain-droplets-on-windshield-close-up-34479250/)
by Jonathan David, accessed 2026-08-04.

One representative 16:9 frame was converted into a grayscale highlight plate
with a black backing. The plate is screen-blended at low opacity, keeping the
real droplet shapes visible without tinting or darkening the user's profile
background. It remains static by design so the pane stays anchored.

The unmodified source video is not shipped.
