# Chromatic Tangle source

Source clip: [Abstract Video](https://www.pexels.com/video/abstract-video-4990320/)
by Luis Quintero, accessed 2026-08-04.

The 1920×1080 source was cropped to a 960×540 light-trail plate. Its black
backdrop disappears under screen blending while the colored trails retain
enough contrast to read as a deliberate moving study rather than a tint. A
forward-and-reverse cycle creates a clean 20-second loop; the poster is a
representative mid-loop frame.
