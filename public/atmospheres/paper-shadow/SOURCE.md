# Paper Shadow source

Source clip: [Close-up Footage of a Crumpled Black Paper](https://www.pexels.com/video/close-up-footage-of-a-crumpled-black-paper-4822908/)
by amine sz, accessed 2026-08-04.

The 16:9 source was converted to a restrained grayscale surface plate. Its
black paper field remains nearly absent under screen blending while the folds
retain enough texture to read as a material rather than a gradient. A
14.5-second forward-and-reverse cycle creates a clean loop boundary; the
poster is a representative mid-loop frame.
