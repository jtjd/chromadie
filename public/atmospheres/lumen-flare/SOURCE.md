# Lumen Flare source

Source clip: [Dynamic Light Flare on Dark Background](https://www.pexels.com/video/dynamic-light-flare-on-dark-background-34645676/)
by Chandresh Uike, accessed 2026-08-04.

The 4K source was center-cropped into a 16:9 grayscale lens-flare plate and
its dark field was suppressed for screen blending. A 9.5-second
forward-and-reverse cycle keeps the flare's rise and fall continuous at the
native loop boundary; the poster is a representative mid-loop frame.
