# Ink Bloom source

Source clip: [Ink in Water](https://www.pexels.com/video/ink-in-water-18333374/)
by Engin Akyurt, accessed 2026-08-04.

The 16:9 source was converted to a restrained black-backed highlight plate so
the ink plume can be screen-blended over a profile without importing the white
water background. A 1.5-second crossfade joins the end back to the start of a
32-second loop. The poster is the reduced-motion fallback.
