# Glass Caustics source

Source clip: [Abstract Water Texture with Light Reflections](https://www.pexels.com/video/abstract-water-texture-with-light-reflections-36303967/)
by Alex Dos Santos, accessed 2026-08-04.

The vertical water source was center-cropped into a 16:9 grayscale caustic
plate. Contrast and a luminance floor retain the refracted highlights while
removing the source's dark field. A 7-second forward-and-reverse cycle creates
a clean loop boundary; the poster is a representative mid-loop frame.
