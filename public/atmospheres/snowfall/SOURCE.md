# Snowfall source

Source clip: [Snowfall in Black Background](https://www.pexels.com/video/snowfall-in-black-background-5485148/)
by Niezlyziom Ozehu, accessed 2026-08-04.

The black-backed 16:9 source was normalized for contrast and encoded as a
40-second loop with a 1.5-second crossfade from its end back into its start.
This keeps the native video loop continuous while preserving the natural
variation in the fall. The poster is the reduced-motion fallback.
