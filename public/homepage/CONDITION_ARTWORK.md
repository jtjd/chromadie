# Homepage collection illustration

## Flat v2 correction

The detailed v1 strip is superseded by three independent transparent assets:
`condition-sum_255-flat-v2.webp`, `condition-sum_69-flat-v2.webp`, and
`condition-sum_42-flat-v2.webp`. Each is optimized to 384px with alpha preserved.
The lower page uses `homepage-lower-atmosphere-v2.webp`, a separate 1400px-wide
painted companion background, rather than a copy of the hero.

Visibility follow-up: the atmosphere is now saved at its native 1024×1536
resolution as lossless WebP. This prevents block compression artifacts in the
dark center when the background is brightened for visibility.

All four were generated with built-in imagegen. Prompt set:

- Disk: simple flat 2D blue floppy disk, frontal square silhouette, dark blue
  outer disk, light blue rectangular shutter with one dark slot, rainbow label
  of four flat stripes. Solid colors, imperfect hand-drawn edges; no texture,
  shading, reflections, bevels, gradients, 3D, text, shadows, or decorations.
- Orbit: two violet oval bands forming an X with three four-point stars,
  dark violet contours, medium violet bands, single flat lavender inset.
  Three-color palette, transparent background, centered square composition;
  no texture, shading, reflections, bevels, gradients, lighting, text, or glow.
- Helix: orange double helix, two flowing bands and five horizontal rungs,
  terracotta contour, orange bands, amber rungs. Frontal silhouette,
  transparent background, centered square composition; no texture, shading,
  reflections, gradients, lighting, bevels, text, shadow, or glow.
- Atmosphere: new tall companion to the hero, same flat anime cloud silhouettes
  and sweeping ribbons at much lower contrast. Near-black #0e0e10 dominates,
  sparse indigo clouds at left/bottom, plum at upper right, faint teal/amber
  edge trails, broad dark central area, charcoal top/bottom for blending.
  No subjects, icons, text, bright spots, or shiny glow.

## Historical v1

`condition-collection-v1.webp` is a decorative illustration for the three
canonical homepage condition examples, not an unlock or catalog definition.
The blue disk, purple orbital motif, and orange helix are presented in that
order above Max Byte, Nice Sum, and Meaning of Life. Labels remain HTML.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool using
`homepage-hero-atmosphere-anime-v1.png` as a style reference. Transparent alpha
is preserved in the optimized 1200px WebP. Original output:
`/home/alex/.codex/generated_images/01a097bb-4c4d-7b23-a75c-3cc18a1e8c26/exec-b03f780b-9576-4b31-85c7-af452d91206f.png`.

Prompt: Create a single transparent illustration for a color-collection game
homepage. Match the reference's flat painterly anime brush texture, ink-dark
contours, vivid painted pigments, and subtle grain. Three separate floating
emblems arranged horizontally with equal spacing: a blue floppy disk with a
rainbow label, interlocking purple orbital rings with tiny violet stars, and a
copper-orange DNA helix with amber painted highlights. Strong silhouettes,
hand-painted flat shading, frontal three-quarter view, all objects contained
at equal visual size. Transparent background; no clouds, scenery, panels,
frames, glow haze, gradients, letters, numbers, or text.
