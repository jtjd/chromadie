# Homepage showcase fixtures

These local backgrounds and portraits are purpose-made showcase
assets for the approved homepage reference. They are deterministic homepage
fixtures only: they do not represent accounts, are not queried from the
database, and cannot enter discovery, leaderboards, or production profile
state.

## Meilin

`meilin/background.webp` and `meilin/avatar.webp` are dedicated, purpose-made
artwork for the first homepage profile specimen. They were generated for this
fictional profile and are not borrowed from profiles 2–4 or from production
user media.
