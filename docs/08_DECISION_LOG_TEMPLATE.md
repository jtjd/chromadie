# Decision Log

Record decisions that affect product direction, architecture, schema, security, or visual language.

## Template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded

**Context**

What problem required a decision?

**Decision**

What was chosen?

**Alternatives considered**

What credible alternatives were rejected?

**Consequences**

What improves, what becomes harder, and what future work is implied?

**Related files**

List relevant implementation and documentation files.
