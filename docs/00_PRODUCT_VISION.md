# Chromadie 2.0 Product Vision

## Product Definition

Chromadie is a social identity game where players build a living digital profile through daily color rolls, collections, accomplishments, customization, and interaction with other people.

The daily roll is the ritual.  
The profile is the destination.  
Discovery is the social loop.  
Customization is the creative loop.  
History is the retention loop.

## Core Promise

A Chromadie profile should feel like stepping into someone's digital room: beautiful, personal, expressive, accumulated over time, and worth sharing.

A visitor should first think:

> This profile is extremely cool.

Then discover:

> This is also a game, and everything here was built through play.

## Primary Outcome

Players become proud enough of their profile to share it voluntarily on Discord, social media, creator pages, communities, and direct messages.

## Product Pillars

### Beauty
Every account begins with an attractive, coherent default profile. Customization improves expression rather than repairing a weak foundation.

### Identity
Players can communicate taste, personality, interests, links, content, and a chosen signature color.

### Story
Rolls, collections, milestones, seasons, rank movement, and social activity leave lasting traces.

### Discovery
Profiles are meaningfully different, so browsing other people becomes an activity rather than a utility.

### Progression
A one-day profile and a two-year profile should feel visibly different without making new users feel punished.

### Sociality
Reactions, follows, guestbook entries, visits, comparisons, and discovery create reasons to return beyond the daily roll.

## Monetization Rule

> Premium unlocks expression. Gameplay unlocks prestige.

Premium may expand creative control, convenience, layouts, media, effects, and module capacity.

Gameplay and participation must remain the source of rank, rare achievements, milestone effects, collection prestige, seasonal recognition, and history.

## Explicit Non-Goals

Chromadie must not become:

- A generic Linktree clone.
- A fakecrime or guns.lol copy.
- An unrestricted HTML-hosting platform.
- A noisy dashboard full of equally weighted statistics.
- A pay-to-look-good product.
- A social network whose content overwhelms the color game.
- A rewrite that discards the secure and working backend.
