Particle-focused prototype batch

Five richer VFX directions

This round is intentionally built around layered particle systems instead of shape stacks. Every effect uses varied particle sizes, lifetimes, motion, and secondary detail so the motion feels more simulated and less like simple lines slapped on top of a card.
